Traditional antivirus is mainly preventive: it checks files against known signatures and heuristics and blocks or quarantines malware before or as it runs. EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response) watches endpoint activity all the time, including processes, network connections, and registry and memory activity, so it can catch suspicious behavior such as fileless or living-off-the-land attacks, give analysts the telemetry to investigate, and let them respond by isolating hosts, killing processes, or rolling back changes.
