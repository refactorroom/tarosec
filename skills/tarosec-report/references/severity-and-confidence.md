# Severity, confidence and remediation

Quote the supplied severity and its rationale. Explain an evidence-backed
disagreement separately. Preserve source confidence, finding status,
verification status, claim strength, incident classification and detection
outcome as distinct fields. The generated JSON has a conservative assessment
confidence of Unknown when evidence is incomplete or unchecked. Review the
actual supporting content before carrying a source conclusion into prose.

Group risk decisions by documented impact, exposure, prerequisites, asset
importance and compensating controls. Explain whether a dependency or urgent
exposure changes remediation priority. Preserve uncertainty about risk when
metadata is missing. Keep false positives visible alongside the reasons they
were closed and exclude them from severity totals used for action planning.

Use evidence-backed impact and mitigation in the audience's terms. For an
unknown score, ownership, attribution, affected asset, execution result, or
coverage measure, leave the field unknown. For proposed verification, describe
the defensive observation or regression condition needed to close the gap.
