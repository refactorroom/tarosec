Purple

Compare expected defensive behavior with recorded exercise results.

All workflowsAuditRedBluePurpleSimulateReport

What to provide

Provide the exercise record, expected telemetry and alerts, and observations from the exercise window.

Modes

plan / exercise
Define a benign exercise and its expected observations.
emulate
Work with prerecorded synthetic activity or a harmless offline test event.
validate
Classify each recorded step.
coverage
Summarize verified opportunities and outcomes.
retest
Compare original and new results by step ID after a change.

Workflow

  1. Separate evidence that a step ran from evidence that a control observed it.
  2. Check the expected rule, attribution, and timing against the supplied observations.
  3. Record an outcome and cite the evidence behind it.

Example agent request

$tarosec purple
Compare my baseline exercise with its retest. Explain each outcome and cite the evidence IDs.

What to expect

Per-step outcomes distinguish detection, late or partial detection, prevention, telemetry or detection gaps, inconclusive evidence, and steps that did not run.
